[box]
🚂 **A timeline = A time train. 時間軸＝時間火車**
每節車廂是一個時間點，依照先後排好。
1. Find the time words. 找時間詞
2. Put them in order. 排順序
3. Write what happened. 寫發生什麼事
4. Use the timeline to tell the story. 用時間軸說故事
📘 **Example 範例：** "In the seventh grade, she sang on stage for the first time." → 時間詞：**in the seventh grade** → 第一站
[/box]

### Part B: Strategy Transfer - A New Story (CAP 短文)
step: 👤 Read the story 1 time. Look at the Word Bank. 讀故事一次，看單字庫。
[box]
**Ming's Robot Dog**
Ming is a college student. He builds robots.
When Ming was ten, he took apart an old radio. From that day, he loved fixing things. He fixed fans and clocks at home.
In 2016, Ming made a robot for a science contest. But the robot stopped working on the stage. Some students laughed at him. Ming was sad, but he didn't stop. He worked on his robot every day.
Two years later, Ming won first prize with his robot dog. The dog can help blind people cross the street.
Now, besides studying, Ming teaches kids to build robots on weekends. He doesn't ask for money.
[/box]
Word Bank: college 大學 / robot 機器人 / take apart 拆開 / contest 比賽 / first prize 第一名 / blind 視障的 / cross 穿越
key: S3 為 S1/S2 同一篇短文的簡化版（約 105 字），自編 CAP-style，非會考原題。

2. When did Ming win first prize? 什麼時候得第一名？　( [[C|3]] )

## Task 3: My Dream Timeline（12 分鐘）
