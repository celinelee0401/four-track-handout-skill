=== card
tag: T1 Strategy Lab
title: 🚂 A timeline = A time train
[box]
Each car is one time. Put the cars in order. 每節車廂是一個時間點，依序排好。
[/box]
- ① Find the time [[words]]. 找時間詞
- ② Put them in [[order]]. 排順序
- ③ Write what [[happened]]. 寫發生什麼事
- ④ Use the timeline to tell the [[story]]. 用時間軸說故事

=== card
tag: T2 Find the Evidence
title: 🔎 IU didn't give up
| What happened? | Evidence (課文證據) |
|---|---|

| But IU … | However, IU didn't [[give up]]. |

[/box]

=== card
tag: T2 Grammar
title: 🧩 decide to V · start V-ing · besides V-ing
| Rule | From the reading |
|---|---|
| decide + **to V** | IU decided [[to become]] a singer. |
| start + **to V / V-ing** | Many people started [[listening]] to her music. |
| besides + **V-ing** | Besides [[singing]], IU also writes songs. |
[box]
❌ IU decided becoming a singer. → ✅ IU decided [[to become]] a singer.
❌ Besides sing, she also acts. → ✅ Besides [[singing]], she also acts.
[/box]

=== card
tag: T2 Part B · Ming's Robot Dog
title: 💡 How to choose the main idea
[box]
A good main idea covers the **whole** timeline, not one stop. 主旨要涵蓋整條時間軸，不是只有一站。
[/box]
| Option | Whole story? |
|---|---|
| Ming's grandpa gave him an old radio. | □ |
| Ming kept trying and became a robot maker who helps others. | [[x]] |
| Ming's robot stopped working at a science contest. | □ |
"He doesn't ask for any money." → He likes [[helping others]].
