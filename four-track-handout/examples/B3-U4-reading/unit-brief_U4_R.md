## 課文（逐字）

**IU's Story**

[Para 1, p. 79] IU is a successful Korean singer. She has fans all over the world. This is her success story.

[Para 2, p. 79] IU found her love for music in junior high school. In the seventh grade, she sang on stage for the first time and decided to become a singer. Her relatives didn't believe in her and made fun of her dream. However, IU didn't give up.

[Para 3, p. 80] IU went to about twenty auditions before a company finally signed her in 2007. Three years later, IU had her first success with her song "Good Day." After that, many people started listening to her music.

[Para 4, p. 80] Now, besides singing, IU also writes songs and acts. Her work is very popular. And that's not all. She often gives her money to those in need. She is a good example for young people.

- 人物：IU（she）、her relatives、a company、young people。
- 寫法（備課用書）：順敘；While You Read 任務「Find the four time periods」。
- 課文大意（備課用書）：IU became a successful singer because she kept trying and never gave up on her dream. Now she is popular and is a good example for young people.

## 文法焦點（本課 Grammar Focus 在課文中的出現）

1. 動詞＋**to V**：decided **to become** a singer。
2. 動詞＋**V-ing**／兩者皆可：started **listening** to her music（start 可接 to V 或 V-ing）。
3. 介系詞＋**V-ing**：**besides singing**。

四軌共用文法操作：從課文找出 decided to become、started listening、besides singing；改錯「IU decided becoming a singer.」→ to become；「Besides sing, …」→ singing。（S1 改錯＋說明、S2 二選一＋句框、S3 圈選、S4 核心句圈選）

## Timeline 策略（四軌共用說明）

比喻：🚂 **A time train（時間火車）**——每一節車廂是一個時間點，依序排好，車廂裡放「發生了什麼事」。
步驟（依備課用書 p. VI）：
1. Find the time words.（找時間詞：in the seventh grade、in 2007、three years later、now）
2. Put them in order.（依先後排好）
3. Write what happened.（每一站寫一句事件）
4. Use the timeline to tell the story.（用時間軸說故事）

### S1/S2 版（約 155 字）— Ming's Robot Dog

Chen Ming lives in Tainan. He is now a college student, and he builds robots. This is his story.
When Ming was ten, his grandpa gave him an old radio. Ming took it apart and put it back together. From that day, he loved fixing things. He fixed his family's fans, clocks, and lamps.
In 2016, Ming made a robot for a science contest, but it stopped working on the stage. Some students laughed at him. Ming felt sad, but he didn't stop. He worked on his robot after school every day. He also read many books about robots.
Two years later, Ming won first prize with his robot dog, "Lucky." The dog can help blind people cross the street. After that, a newspaper wrote a story about him.
Now, besides studying at college, Ming teaches kids to build robots on weekends. He doesn't ask for any money. He wants more kids to love science.

Q1 (B) main idea｜Q2 (C) in 2018｜Q3 (A) b→c→a→d｜Q4 (B) He likes helping others.
Timeline：when he was ten — took apart an old radio, loved fixing things｜2016 — robot stopped working at the contest｜2018 (two years later) — won first prize with robot dog Lucky｜now — studies at college, teaches kids on weekends for free.
