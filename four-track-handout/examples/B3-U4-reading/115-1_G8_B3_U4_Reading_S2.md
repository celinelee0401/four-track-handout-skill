# Part 2: Reading - IU's Story
Goal (目標): I can find time words (時間詞) and put events (事件) on a timeline (時間軸). I can compare IU with Yuki and decide if talent (天分) is enough for a dream. I can plan my own dream timeline. I can ask my own questions (提問) and check what AI says with the text.
