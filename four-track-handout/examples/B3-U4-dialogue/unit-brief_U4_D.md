Unit 4: What Do You Want to Be in the Future?｜課本 pp. 69–70｜CD2: 20–23（單字 CD2: 24）
來源：`5.課本習作/2-1學用課本PDF/115 iE3 課本_U4+R2(學用).pdf`（文字與插圖已核對）、教用課本（B 解答）、`備課用書2-u4+r2+節慶.pdf`（翻譯、課文解析、Pre/Post-Listening Questions）。

## 課文（逐字）

(In the classroom)
Cody: What are you doing, Zac?
Zac: I'm making a video for my YouTube channel.
Yuki: How cool!
Zac: I enjoy making videos, and I hope to be a famous YouTuber one day.
Yuki: And you, Cody? What do you want to be in the future?
Cody: I plan to be a lawyer. I want to help people in need and fight for their rights.
Yuki: Good for you.
Cody: What's your dream job, Yuki?
Yuki: Being a singer is my dream job.
Zac: It's great to have a dream. And you're really good at singing.
Yuki: But I hate singing in front of people. I get butterflies in my stomach when people watch me.
Zac: Don't worry. Just keep practicing.
Cody: Or you can put on a big wig, and then you can't see people.
Yuki: Haha. Good idea.

- 人物：Cody（男，藍上衣）、Zac（男，捲髮綠上衣）、Yuki（女，黃上衣）。地點：教室。
- 情境（備課用書）：Cody, Zac, and Yuki are talking about their dream jobs.

## 插圖（T1 See-Think-Wonder 依據）

- p. 69：Zac 用自拍棒拿手機拍影片；泡泡裡是 Zac 的頻道畫面（戴耳機的兩個人、筆電）。Cody 雙手交叉，想著三位穿西裝的律師（一人拿書）。Yuki 在旁邊聽。
- p. 70 左：泡泡裡 Yuki 拿麥克風在聚光燈下唱歌，用紅色扇子遮住臉；Yuki 雙手摸臉、很害羞／緊張；Zac 拿著平板和麥克風對著她。
- p. 70 右：Cody 舉起手指（有點子）；泡泡裡 Yuki 戴著超大橘色假髮在唱歌，旁邊有音符。
- Think 推論焦點：Yuki 為什麼用扇子遮臉？→ 她很緊張（get butterflies）。（備課用書 Pre-Listening Q4：Maybe she is nervous.）

## 文法焦點（Grammar Focus 1–2、Usage 1–2）

1. 動詞＋**to V**：want / hope / plan / need to V（I hope **to be** …／I plan **to be** …／I want **to help** …）。
2. 動詞＋**V-ing**：enjoy / keep / practice / finish V-ing（I enjoy **making** videos.／Just keep **practicing**.）。like / love / hate 兩者皆可（I hate **singing** …）。
3. 動名詞當主詞：**Being** a singer is my dream job.
4. 補充：be good at **V-ing**（介系詞＋V-ing）；It's great **to have** a dream.（虛主詞 it）

四軌共用文法操作：從對話找出「動詞＋to V」與「動詞＋V-ing」並分類；判斷「Zac enjoys to make videos.」對不對（S1 改寫＋說明、S2 二選一＋句框、S3 圈選、S4 核心句圈選）。

## 三個原文核心句（四軌 T2／T4 與 S4 共用）

1. I enjoy making videos, and I hope to be a famous YouTuber one day.
2. I plan to be a lawyer.
3. Being a singer is my dream job.

## T3 主題

- **SEL｜Butterflies? Keep Practicing!（緊張也沒關係，持續練習）**：Yuki 很會唱歌、有夢想，卻在別人面前緊張；朋友給她鼓勵與點子。理由：對話轉折正是「有夢想 → 遇到緊張 → 朋友支持＋自己練習」，連結自我管理（面對緊張）與成長型思維（keep practicing），也連到備課用書 Post-Listening Q4（怎麼克服在人前表現的恐懼）。
- **Part A（SEL 反思）**：什麼時候你會 get butterflies？給 Yuki 一個新點子。
- **Part B 4/3/2 說話題目（四軌同題）**：**My Dream Job** — What do you want to be in the future? What do you enjoy doing? What do you need to practice? 用本課剛練的 want to / enjoy V-ing / need to / keep V-ing（已學語言）。
