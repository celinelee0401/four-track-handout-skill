=== card
tag: T1 Our Summary
title: 📝 The Summary (課本 p. 59 C)
[box]
Emma had [[bad dreams]] almost every night. [[Nothing]] helped. Then her friend gave her a [[dreamcatcher]], and it worked. Emma could finally [[sleep through the night]].
[/box]
✅ Beginning ＋ Middle ＋ End　　✅ No cake, no bear　　✅ 4 short sentences

=== card
tag: T2 Grammar
title: ⏳ Long action + when + short action
| Long action 長動作（正在進行） | when | Short action 短動作（突然發生） |
|---|---|---|
| She [[was eating]] cake | when | her teeth [[fell]] out. |
| A bear [[was running]] after her | when | she [[woke]] up. |
[box]
長動作：**was / were + V-ing**　　短動作：**過去式**
I / he / she / Emma → {{was* / were}}　　you / we / they → {{was / were*}}
[/box]

=== card
tag: T2 Reading Clue
title: 🔄 However = the opposite
[box]
Her parents gave her some tips. 👍 　**However**, 　nothing worked. 👎
[/box]
- However 前後兩件事的意思 {{same 相同 / opposite 相反*}}。
- However 很像 [[but|6]]，但放在句子開頭，後面加逗號。
- 在 Noisy Nights 裡：He closed the window and wore headphones. However, [[none of these ideas helped]].

=== card
tag: T2 Find the Evidence
title: 🔎 Answers from the text
- When did the bad dreams start? → After she [[moved to a new city]]. (Para 1)
- Why was Emma tired? → The dreams [[woke her up]], and she was [[scared]]. (Para 1)
- What does a dreamcatcher do? → The web catches the [[bad]] dreams. The [[good]] ones go through the holes. (Para 3)
- Who might need a dreamcatcher? → [[Riley]]. She often has bad dreams and wakes up many times.
