## 課文（逐字）

**The Dreamcatcher**

[Para 1, p. 57] After Emma moved to a new city, she had bad dreams almost every night. In one dream, she was eating cake when her teeth fell out one by one. In another, a bear was running after her. These dreams always woke her up. After that, she couldn't fall asleep again because she was scared. She couldn't get enough sleep and was tired all the time.

[Para 2, p. 58] Emma told her family and friends about her problem. "You're just nervous about your new school," her parents said and gave her some tips. However, nothing worked.

[Para 3, p. 58] One day, her friend Dakota gave her a dreamcatcher. "Hang this by your bed. The web catches the bad dreams, but the good ones go through the holes," he told her. Emma hung it up, and it worked! Finally, she could sleep through the night. The dreamcatcher saved her from terrible nights.

- 人物：Emma、her parents、her friend Dakota (he)。地點：a new city；物：dreamcatcher。
- 結構（備課用書）：觸發／困境（P1）→ 嘗試失敗（P2）→ 解決（P3）。
- Reading Clue：however 連接意思相反的兩句（父母給 tips ↔ nothing worked）。
- While You Read：What does a dreamcatcher do? → It catches bad dreams.

## 文法焦點（課本 Grammar Focus 1–2，課文中出現）

1. 過去進行式 was / were + V-ing：a bear **was running** after her.
2. 過去進行式 ＋ when ＋ 過去簡單式：she **was eating** cake **when** her teeth **fell** out one by one.

四軌共用文法操作：Part A 第 2 題「What was Emma doing when her teeth fell out?」→ She was eating cake.（S1/S2 再加：`A bear was running after Emma.` + `She woke up.` 用 when 合併。）

## 摘要策略（四軌共用說明）

比喻：📦 **Pack a small bag**（打包小背包）——只帶重要的東西，丟掉小東西。

## T3 主題

- **SEL｜Be Someone's Dakota（當別人的 Dakota）**：Emma 搬家後很緊張、睡不好；家人給的方法沒用，但朋友 Dakota 的一個小幫忙讓她走出來。理由：課文正是「新環境的壓力 → 求助 → 朋友支持」的 SEL 歷程（自我覺察＋人際支持），也連結備課用書「減少惡夢的方法」。
- **成品（四軌同）**：寫一張 **Help Note** 給一位剛轉學來、很緊張、晚上睡不好的新同學：(1) 說你懂他的感受；(2) 給 2 個幫助的方法（你的 "dreamcatcher"）；(3) 一句鼓勵。
- 鷹架：S1 60–80 字開放＋連接詞；S2 organizer＋引導問題 40–60 字；S3 句框＋Idea Bank 3 句；S4 勾選方法＋1–2 句句框。完成後 📢 唸給夥伴聽、夥伴簽名。

### S1/S2 版（約 155 字）— Noisy Nights

Last summer, Ryan's family moved into an apartment near a busy night market. His room was small, and his desk was next to the window. Every night, Ryan was trying to study when loud music came through his window. People were talking and laughing until midnight. Ryan couldn't think, and his test scores went down.

He tried many things. He closed the window, but his room got too hot. He wore headphones, but he could still hear the music. However, none of these ideas helped.

One afternoon, his neighbor Mrs. Lin saw him with his heavy books. "Come to the library with me after school," she said. "It's open until nine, and it's very quiet." Ryan went with her the next day. He sat by a big window and finished all his homework in two hours.

Now Ryan studies at the library every evening and goes home after nine. His test scores are going up again.

Q1 (B) best summary｜Q2 (C) studying｜Q3 (B) couldn't think because of the noise｜Q4 (C) He sat by a big window.（不重要的細節）
Story map：Beginning — loud night market / couldn't study；Middle — closed window, headphones → nothing helped；End — Mrs. Lin → library → scores up.
