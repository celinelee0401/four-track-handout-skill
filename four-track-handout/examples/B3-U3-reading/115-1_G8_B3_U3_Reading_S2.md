# Part 2: Reading - The Dreamcatcher
Goal (目標): I can find the main idea (大意) of each paragraph. I can cut small details (細節) and write a short summary (摘要). I can help a friend with a worry (擔心).

## Task 1: Strategy Lab - Summarizing (摘要)
flow: 👤🧠 自學思考 → 👥 討論 → 📢 表達 → 📋 統整
step: 👤 Listen to "The Dreamcatcher" 1 time. (CD2: 8-11)
step: 👤 Read pp. 57-58. Then read the box and the example (例子).
[box]
📦 **Summarizing = Pack a small bag.** (摘要＝打包小背包) Take only the big things. Leave the small things at home.
1. Find the main idea of each paragraph. (找每段大意)
2. Put the ideas together. (連起來)
3. Cut the small details. (刪掉小細節)
4. Say it in your own words. (用自己的話說)
📘 **Example (範例):** "In one dream, she was eating cake when her teeth fell out." → This is an example of a bad dream. It is a small detail. ✂️ Cut it!
[/box]

### Part B: Strategy Transfer - A New Story (CAP 短文)

[box]
**Noisy Nights**
Last summer, Ryan's family moved into an apartment near a busy night market. His room was small, and his desk was next to the window. Every night, Ryan was trying to study when loud music came through his window. People were talking and laughing until midnight. Ryan couldn't think, and his test scores went down.
He tried many things. He closed the window, but his room got too hot. He wore headphones, but he could still hear the music. However, none of these ideas helped.
One afternoon, his neighbor Mrs. Lin saw him with his heavy books. "Come to the library with me after school," she said. "It's open until nine, and it's very quiet." Ryan went with her the next day. He sat by a big window and finished all his homework in two hours.
Now Ryan studies at the library every evening and goes home after nine. His test scores are going up again.
[/box]
Word Bank: apartment (n.) 公寓 / night market (n.) 夜市 / loud (adj.) 大聲的 / midnight (n.) 午夜 / score (n.) 分數 / headphones (n.) 耳機 / neighbor (n.) 鄰居 / heavy (adj.) 重的

## Task 1: Strategy Lab - Summarizing（15 分鐘）
- 目的：依引導問題寫出三段大意，判斷 Keep/Cut，再用句框組成摘要。
- 步驟：播 CD2: 8–11 一次 → 帶讀策略框與範例（cake 為何刪）→ Story Map 5 分 → Keep/Cut 3 分 → 句框摘要 → 兩兩互讀。
- 📋 統整：黑板三格 Story Map；與課本 p. 59 B、C 答案核對（bad dreams / dreamcatcher / sleep through）。
- 常見錯誤：Middle 寫 her parents said「You're just nervous…」（是細節）；sleep through 漏 through。

## Task 2: Strategy Practice（20 分鐘）
- 目的：同 S1——細節到推論的問題梯、文法 was + V-ing ＋ when、用摘要策略讀 Part B。
- 步驟：Part A 句框作答 8 分 → 兩兩指出課本證據 → Grammar 填空＋圈長短動作；Part B 先看 Word Bank 再讀 6 分 → 作答 → 兩兩討論第 4 題。
- 📋 統整：①長動作 was running ＋ when ＋ 短動作 woke；② however 表相反；③ Part B 1B 2C 3B 4C，摘要題用「是否涵蓋開頭＋解決」刪去 A、C、D。
- 常見錯誤：Q2 寫 ate cake；Q4 圈 same；Part B Q2 選 A（只看到 midnight）。

## Task 3: Be Someone's Dakota（15 分鐘）
- 目的：SEL——同理新同學的緊張，給出具體、有理由的支持方法。
- 步驟：思考題句框 3 分 → 計畫表只寫關鍵字 3 分 → Help Note 40–60 字 6 分 → 交換、唸給 2 位夥伴。
- 📋 統整：全班「dreamcatcher」方法清單；示範 First … because … / Also …。
- 常見錯誤：計畫表寫成整句（請改關鍵字）；because 後面接名詞（because music → because it helps me relax）。
