# Part 2: Reading - The Dreamcatcher
Goal (目標): I can find the big idea of each paragraph. 我能找出每段的重點，刪掉小細節，完成一段摘要，並寫一張小卡幫助朋友。

1. Find the main idea of each paragraph. 找每段大意

### Part B: Strategy Transfer - A New Story (CAP 短文)

**Noisy Nights**
Ryan's family moved to an apartment near a busy night market. Every night, Ryan was studying when loud music came in. People were loud until midnight. Ryan couldn't think. His test scores went down.
He closed the window, but his room got too hot. He wore headphones, but he could still hear the music. However, nothing helped.
One day, his neighbor Mrs. Lin said, "Come to the library with me. It's very quiet." Ryan went with her. He sat by a big window and finished his homework fast.
Now Ryan studies at the library every evening. His test scores are going up again.
[/box]

📩 **Situation 情境：** A new student, Amy, just moved to our class. She is nervous. She can't sleep well at night. 新同學 Amy 剛轉來，很緊張，晚上睡不好。

step: 🧠 Check why the gift worked. 勾選為什麼禮物有用。
Why did Dakota's gift work? 為什麼 Dakota 的禮物有用？ Check 1 or more. 勾一個以上。
□ Emma felt safe. (覺得安心)　□ A friend helped her. (朋友幫忙)　□ It was very expensive. (很貴)

## Task 1: Strategy Lab - Summarizing（15 分鐘）
- 目的：用 6 字 Word Bank 完成三段大意，勾選 Keep/Cut，圈出摘要關鍵字並一起說出。
- 步驟：播 CD2: 8–11 一次 → 帶讀策略框（打包小背包）→ Story Map 4 分 → Keep/Cut 2 分 → 圈摘要 → 全組齊說。
- 📋 統整：黑板三格 Story Map（與 S1/S2 同）；全班齊讀課本 p. 59 C 摘要。
- 常見錯誤：Word Bank 的 parents 與 nothing 放錯格；Keep/Cut 把「熊在追她」勾 Keep（提醒它是惡夢的例子）。

## Task 2: Strategy Practice（20 分鐘）
- 目的：同 S1/S2 的問題梯（改選擇／勾選），操作 was + V-ing；用摘要策略讀簡化版 Part B。
- 步驟：Part A 7 分（每題在課本畫證據）→ 兩兩核對 → Grammar 圈選；Part B 讀 5 分 → 3 題 → Story Map。
- 📋 統整：Part A 1B 2A 3（夢吵醒、害怕）4C 5 bad/good 6C；Part B 1B 2C 3C；文法：was ＋ V-ing，when 後面用過去式 fell。
- 常見錯誤：Grammar 3 選 were falling；Part B 第 3 題選 A（以為第一句都是細節）。

## Task 3: Be Someone's Dakota（12 分鐘）
- 目的：SEL——用 Idea Bank 給新同學 2 個具體方法並鼓勵。
- 步驟：勾理由 → 勾 2 個方法 → 寫 3 句 → 唸給夥伴、再唸給 2 位並簽名。
- 📋 統整：全班一起說 You are not alone!；挑 2 句 First, you can … 示範。
- 常見錯誤：First, you can listening…（can 後面用原形）。
