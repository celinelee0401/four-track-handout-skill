[box]
**Noisy Nights**
Last summer, Ryan's family moved into an apartment near a busy night market. His room was small, and his desk was next to the window. Every night, Ryan was trying to study when loud music came through his window. People were talking and laughing until midnight. Ryan couldn't think, and his test scores went down.
He tried many things. He closed the window, but his room got too hot. He wore headphones, but he could still hear the music. However, none of these ideas helped.
One afternoon, his neighbor Mrs. Lin saw him with his heavy books. "Come to the library with me after school," she said. "It's open until nine, and it's very quiet." Ryan went with her the next day. He sat by a big window and finished all his homework in two hours.
Now Ryan studies at the library every evening and goes home after nine. His test scores are going up again.
[/box]

=== TEACHER GUIDE ===
適用：S1（全英文、無 Word Bank；要求自己寫段落大意、判斷 Keep/Cut、說明 however 功能與文法長短動作）。課本 pp. 57–58，CD2: 8–11。

## Task 1: Strategy Lab - Summarizing（15 分鐘）
- 目的：用「找段落大意 → 連起來 → 刪細節 → 用自己的話說」四步，把三段故事濃縮成 30–45 字摘要。
- 步驟：播 CD2: 8–11 一次 → 學生自讀並完成 Story Map（5 分）→ Keep/Cut（3 分）→ 寫摘要並數字數 → 兩兩互讀 → 請 1–2 位分享。
- 📋 統整：黑板三格 Story Map（Beginning：new city + bad dreams／Middle：nothing worked／End：dreamcatcher → sleep through the night），再對照課本 p. 59 C 範例摘要。
- 常見錯誤：把 cake、bear 寫進摘要（是例子，不是大意）；Middle 只寫「parents said…」引號原句，提醒改成自己的話（Her family tried to help, but nothing worked.）。

## Task 2: Strategy Practice（20 分鐘）
- 目的：Part A 由細節到推論回答課文問題，並操作本課文法（was eating … when … fell）；Part B 用同一摘要策略讀一篇陌生 CAP-style 短文。
- 步驟：Part A 個人 8 分 → 兩兩核對並指出課本證據 → Grammar 合併句；Part B 計時 6 分讀＋作答 → 兩兩討論第 4 題（為什麼 C 是細節）。
- 📋 統整：①長動作 was/were + V-ing ＋ when ＋ 短動作過去式；② however = 前後相反；③ Part B 答案 1B 2C 3B 4C，並示範「選摘要題」的刪去法：A、C、D 都只講一個細節。
- 常見錯誤：Q2 寫成 She ate cake.（應為 was eating）；Q4 只翻譯 however 卻沒說前後哪兩件事相反；Part B Q1 選 A（只看第一句）。

## Task 3: Be Someone's Dakota（15 分鐘）
- 目的：SEL——覺察新環境的緊張，學會給予具體支持；遷移課文「朋友的一個幫忙」。
- 步驟：思考題 2 題（4 分）→ Help Note 60–80 字（8 分）→ 交換找一個好點子 → 唸給 2 位夥伴並簽名。
- 📋 統整：黑板收集全班「dreamcatcher」方法（soft music、write worries down、talk to someone、no phone before bed…），選 2 個最具體的句子示範。
- 常見錯誤：只寫 Don't be nervous.（沒有方法）；方法沒有理由，提醒加 because / so。

## Task 4: Reading Aloud Evaluation（課後）
- 評分重點：全文清楚、語調（引號內的對話要有語氣）、was eating / was running 不吞字、30 秒摘要不看稿且有開頭—過程—結尾。
