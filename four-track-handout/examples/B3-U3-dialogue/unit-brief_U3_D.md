## 課文（逐字）

(On Saturday morning)
Kim: Someone broke my favorite cup.
Jamie: Maybe Lucky did it.
Kim: Let's check the video from the dog monitor.
Jamie: Stop! Mom was doing the dishes at half past twelve.
Kim: It was her. I knew it.
Jamie: Take it easy, Kim. Look. The cup was fine when she left.
Kim: All right.
(Five minutes later)
Kim: Hey, what were you doing in the kitchen at a quarter after one? It was you.
Jamie: Cool down. I was drinking water, and I wasn't even close to the table. See?
Kim: Then who broke my cup?
Jamie: Er, look. You did. You were in the kitchen at 2 a.m.
Kim: I was in the kitchen? I don't remember that at all.
Jamie: Your eyes weren't open. What were you doing?
Kim: Oh no! I was sleepwalking when I broke my cup.

## 插圖（T1 See-Think-Wonder 依據）

- p. 49 左：Kim（雙馬尾、粉紅上衣）拿著破掉的藍色杯子，很生氣；Jamie（星星上衣）摸著狗 Lucky；桌上有書、平板、藍杯。
- p. 49 右：平板畫面是 Mom 穿圍裙在水槽洗碗，旁邊有藍杯；Kim 生氣，桌上有狗狗監視器（攝影機）。
- p. 50 左：畫面是 Jamie 在廚房喝水；Kim 指著 Jamie，Jamie 攤手。
- p. 50 右：畫面是 Kim 閉著眼、雙手往前（夢遊）碰到杯子；兩人和狗都很驚訝。

## 文法焦點

1. 過去進行式：`was / were + V-ing`（某個過去時間點正在做的事）。主詞配對：I/he/she/it/Mom → was；you/we/they → were。
2. `過去進行式 + when + 過去簡單式`：長動作（背景）進行中，短動作發生。I was sleepwalking when I broke my cup.

四軌共用文法操作：把 `Kim was sleepwalking.` + `She broke her cup.` 用 when 合併，並判斷 was / were。

## T3 主題

- **SEL｜先冷靜、再查證（Take it easy & check the facts）**：Kim 兩次沒看完證據就怪人（It was her. / It was you.），最後發現是自己。理由：本課對話的轉折正是「急著怪人 → 證據 → 自己」。
- **4/3/2 說話題目（四軌同題）**：My Last Night Timeline — What were you doing at 7 p.m., 9 p.m., and 11 p.m. last night? 使用本課剛練過的過去進行式（已學語言）。
