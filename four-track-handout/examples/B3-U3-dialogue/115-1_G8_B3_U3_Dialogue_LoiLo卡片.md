=== card
tag: T2 Grammar
title: 🧩 was or were?
| Subject 主詞 | was / were | + V-ing |
|---|---|---|
| I / he / she / it / Mom | {{was* / were}} | doing the dishes |
| you / we / they | {{was / were*}} | drinking water |
| Jamie and the dog | {{was / were*}} | sleeping |
[box]
❌ Mom was **do** the dishes. → ✅ Mom was [[doing@last]] the dishes.
[/box]

=== card
tag: T2 Grammar
title: ⏳ Long action + when + short action
| Long action 長動作（正在進行） | when | Short action 短動作（突然發生） |
|---|---|---|
| I [[was sleepwalking]] | when | I [[broke]] my cup. |
| They [[were cooking]] | when | I [[walked]] into the kitchen. |
[box]
長動作：**was / were + V-ing**　　短動作：**過去式**
❌ I was sleepwalking when I was breaking my cup. → ✅ … when I [[broke@last]] my cup.
[/box]
