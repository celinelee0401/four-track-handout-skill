step: 🧠 Answer Questions 1-4 in B. Use full sentences.
### ■ B. Think Like a Detective (偵探推理)
1. Kim thought three "people" broke the cup. Who were they? Write them in order.
① [[Lucky|10]]  →  ② [[Mom|10]]  →  ③ [[Jamie|10]]
2. Mom did not break the cup. How do you know? Write the words from the Dialogue.
[[The cup was fine when she left.|46]]
3. Jamie did not break the cup. How do you know?
[[She wasn't even close to the table.|46]]
4. Why doesn't Kim remember it?
[[Her eyes weren't open. She was sleepwalking.|52]]

step: 🧠 Do Questions 1-3 in C.
### ■ C. Grammar: was / were + V-ing and when
1. Put the two sentences together. Use **when**.
Kim was sleepwalking. + She broke her cup.
[[Kim was sleepwalking when she broke her cup.|56]]
2. Look at your sentence. Which action was long? Which action was short? Why do we use "was sleepwalking" but "broke"?
lines: 2 | "Was sleepwalking" is a long action. It was happening. "Broke" is a short action. It happened in the middle of the long action. | 說出長動作（進行中）與短動作（突然發生）即可
3. Find the mistake. Write the right sentence.
"At 1:15, Jamie and the dog was sleeping in the kitchen."
[[At 1:15, Jamie and the dog were sleeping in the kitchen.|56]]
key: 第 3 題：主詞 Jamie and the dog 是複數 → were。（句子內容是文法練習，不是課文事實。）

step: 👤 Open your book to pp. 49-50. Find the words for your answers.
step: 👥 Check A, B, and C with a partner. Fix your answers.
step: 📋 Listen to your teacher. Check the time line.

=== TEACHER GUIDE ===
適用：S1（全英文、無 Word Bank、要求推理與文法說明）。課本 pp. 49–50，CD2: 1–4。

## Task 1: See-Think-Wonder（10 分鐘）
- 目的：從四張圖預測「誰打破杯子」，先製造懸念，讓 Task 2 聽力有目的。
- 步驟：學生先蓋住文字只看圖 3 分鐘 → 兩兩找一個不同想法 → 每組分享一個 Think。
- 📋 統整：把全班猜的嫌疑人（Lucky / Mom / Jamie / Kim）寫在黑板上，先不公布答案。
- 常見錯誤：See 寫成猜測（She is angry because…）。提醒 See 只寫看得到的事。

## Task 2: Dialogue Listening（15 分鐘）
- 目的：聽懂時間線與證據，並操作本課文法 was/were + V-ing、when。
- 步驟：播 CD2: 1–4 兩次（闔書）→ 開書找原句 → 兩兩核對 → 全班完成黑板時間線。
- 📋 統整：黑板畫時間線 12:30 Mom / 1:15 Jamie / 2:00 Kim；再寫 was/were 主詞配對表與「長動作 was V-ing ＋ when ＋ 短動作 過去式」。
- 常見錯誤：was do the dishes（漏 -ing）；Jamie and the dog was（複數主詞用 was）；把 when 子句也寫成進行式。

## Task 3: Take It Easy & 4-3-2（15 分鐘）
- 目的：SEL——先冷靜、再查證；並用剛學的過去進行式流暢說自己的事。
- 步驟：Part A 個人寫 3 分鐘 → 兩兩分享；Part B Speaking Map 只寫關鍵字 2 分鐘 → 三輪 35/30/25 秒，換三位夥伴。
- 📋 統整：請 2 位學生說出 when 句（I was … when …），全班複述。
- 常見錯誤：在 Speaking Map 寫整句（請改成關鍵字）；說話時一直看稿。

## Task 4: Speaking Evaluation（課後）
- 目的：完整朗讀對話（語調、角色聲音）＋ 30 秒重述案件。
- 評分重點：清楚度、兩種聲音、正確使用 was/were + V-ing、30 秒完整。

## Task 5: Dialogue Explanation（課後）
- 目的：影片複習時間表達（half past / a quarter after）與過去進行式；學生手寫筆記上傳。
- 下一堂：依筆記抽查 was/were 與 when 句型。
