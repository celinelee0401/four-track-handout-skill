=== card

title: 🧩 U4 文法卡：to V 和 V-ing
文法課專用 · 對照 115-1 G8 Handouts「文法解析」pp. 60-66
壹 第二個動詞 p. 60 · 貳 當受詞 pp. 60-64 · 參 V-ing 當主詞 pp. 64-65 · 肆 虛主詞 It pp. 65-66
例句先用課文：Zac、Cody、Yuki（Dialogue）和 IU（Reading）

=== card
tag: 壹 第二個動詞｜p. 60
title: 🚂 一句話只有一個「主要動詞」
[box]
第二個動詞出現時，要變成 **to V**（不定詞）或 **V-ing**（動名詞）。
[/box]
| 課文句子 | 主要動詞 | 第二個動詞 |
|---|---|---|
| I hope to be a famous YouTuber. (Zac) | [[hope]] | [[to be]] |
| I enjoy making videos. (Zac) | [[enjoy]] | [[making]] |
| IU decided to become a singer. (Reading) | [[decided]] | [[to become]] |
- ❌ I hope be a famous YouTuber. → ✅ I hope [[to be@last]] a famous YouTuber.

=== card
tag: 貳-一 動詞 + to V｜p. 60
compact: true
title: 🎯 想要、計畫、決定 → 還沒做的事 → to V
| 句型 | 課文例句 |
|---|---|
| want / need / plan / hope / decide / learn / agree ＋ **to V** | Cody: I plan [[to be]] a lawyer. |
| | Cody: I want [[to help]] people in need. |
| | IU decided [[to become]] a singer. |
| ask / tell / teach / invite ＋ **人** ＋ **to V** | Zac told Yuki [[to keep]] practicing. |
[box]
小撇步：to V 常常是「往前看」——想做、要做、決定要做。
[/box]

=== card
tag: 貳-一 動詞 + to V｜p. 60
title: 🔧 找錯、改正、說出為什麼
- ❌ Yuki needs practicing more. → ✅ Yuki needs [[to practice]] more.
- ❌ She hopes singing on a big stage. → ✅ She hopes [[to sing]] on a big stage.
- ❌ IU decided to not give up. → ✅ IU decided [[not to give up]].
[box]
Why? need、hope、decide 後面接 [[to V@last]]；否定的 not 放在 [[to 前面@last]]。
[/box]

=== card
tag: 貳-二 動詞 + V-ing｜pp. 61-62
title: 🎬 喜歡、繼續、練習、完成 → V-ing
| 句型 | 課文例句 |
|---|---|
| enjoy / keep / practice / finish / be busy / mind / quit / avoid ＋ **V-ing** | Zac: I enjoy [[making]] videos. |
| | Zac: Just keep [[practicing]]. |
| 介系詞（good at / afraid of / about / for / without / besides）＋ **V-ing** | You're really good at [[singing]]. |
| | Besides [[singing]], IU also writes songs. |
[box]
介系詞後面要接名詞，動詞要先變成 V-ing 才能當名詞用。
[/box]

=== card
tag: 貳-二 動詞 + V-ing｜pp. 61-62
title: 🔧 找錯、改正、說出為什麼
- ❌ Zac enjoys to make videos. → ✅ Zac enjoys [[making]] videos.
- ❌ Yuki is good at sing. → ✅ Yuki is good at [[singing]].
- ❌ Besides sing, IU also acts. → ✅ Besides [[singing]], IU also acts.
[box]
Why? enjoy 後面接 [[V-ing@last]]；good at、besides 是介系詞，後面也接 [[V-ing@last]]。
[/box]

=== card
tag: 貳-三 兩種都可以，意思一樣｜p. 63
title: 🔁 like / love / hate / start / begin
| 課文句子 | 換一種說法（意思一樣） |
|---|---|
| Yuki: I hate singing in front of people. | I hate [[to sing]] in front of people. |
| Many people started listening to her music. | Many people started [[to listen]] to her music. |
[box]
這五個字後面接 to V 或 V-ing 都可以，**意思不變**。
[/box]

=== card
tag: 貳-四 兩種都可以，意思不同｜p. 64
title: ⚠️ remember / forget / stop / try
| 動詞 | ＋ to V（還沒做、要去做） | ＋ V-ing（做過、正在做） |
|---|---|---|
| remember | 記得 [[要去做]] | 記得 [[做過]] |
| forget | 忘記 [[要去做]] | 忘記 [[做過]] |
| stop | 停下來 [[去做另一件事]] | 停止 [[正在做的事]] |
| try | [[努力]] 試著做 | 試試看（找方法） |
- （自編）Yuki, remember [[to take]] a deep breath before you sing. （還沒唱，提醒她要去做）
- （自編）Yuki stopped [[singing]] and took a deep breath. （停止正在唱的歌）

=== card
tag: 參 V-ing 當主詞｜pp. 64-65
title: 👑 V-ing 放在句首，當「主詞」
[box]
**Being a singer** is my dream job. （Yuki）
主詞 = [[Being a singer]]　　動詞 = [[is]]
[/box]
- ❌ Be a singer is my dream job. → ✅ [[Being]] a singer is my dream job.
- 一件事 → 單數：Making videos [[is]] fun.
- 兩件事 → 複數：Making videos and singing [[are]] fun.

=== card
tag: 肆 虛主詞 It｜pp. 65-66
title: 🎈 主詞太長？先用 It 頂替，真正的主詞放後面
| 課文句子 | 換成 V-ing 當主詞 |
|---|---|
| Zac: It's great to have a dream. | [[Having]] a dream is great. |
| It is hard for Yuki to sing in front of people. | [[Singing]] in front of people is hard for Yuki. |
- It is ＋ 形容詞 ＋ **for** 人 ＋ to V：It is hard [[for]] Yuki to sing in front of people.
- 說人的個性（nice / kind）用 **of**：It is kind [[of]] IU to give money to those in need.

=== card
tag: 總整理｜pp. 60-66
title: 🗺️ 第二個動詞怎麼變？
| 只接 to V | 只接 V-ing | 兩種都可（意思一樣） | 兩種都可（意思不同） |
|---|---|---|---|
| want, need, [[plan]], hope, [[decide]] | enjoy, [[keep]], practice, finish | like, love, [[hate]], start, begin | remember, forget, [[stop]], try |
[box]
介系詞（good at、besides）＋ [[V-ing@last]]　　V-ing 當主詞 → 一件事用 [[is@last]]
[/box]
